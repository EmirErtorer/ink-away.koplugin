# Ink Away privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Ink Away is a free plugin for KOReader. It runs on your e-reader. I have no server, no accounts of my own, no analytics and no advertising, and I do not collect, receive or store any information about you or your files.

Contact: emir1.ertorer@gmail.com

## What stays on your reader

Your notebooks, drawings, book notes, settings and the safety copies Ink Away makes are files on your reader. They never leave it unless you turn on cloud sync or send something yourself.

## Cloud sync (optional, off until you connect)

If you connect Google Drive or Dropbox, Ink Away copies the parts you choose under "What to sync" (notebooks and drawings, book notes, ink on book pages, imported PDFs and pictures, exports, screen clips, and some preferences) between your readers, through your own cloud account.

- **How you sign in.** You sign in on the cloud service's own page, on your phone. Ink Away never sees your password. The service gives your reader an access key.
- **Where the key is kept.** On your reader only, in a file in KOReader's data folder (in a folder called "inkaway-sync", outside your notebooks, so it is never synced) that only the reader's own user can read. It is not encrypted, so anyone who can open the reader's files, over USB for instance, could read it, as with KOReader's other settings. It is not sent to me or anyone else. "Disconnect" deletes it.
- **What Ink Away can reach.** Only its own folder: "Ink Away" in My Drive (Google Drive), "Apps/Ink Away" (Dropbox). It cannot see anything else in your account.
- **Where your data goes.** Straight from your reader to your cloud account, and back. It does not pass through any server of mine.
- **What Ink Away reads from your account.** Your name and email address, to show which account is connected on the reader. They are kept on the reader and used for nothing else.
- **Deleting and disconnecting.** "Disconnect" in Cloud sync forgets the account on the reader and leaves your files in the cloud. To delete the files, delete the "Ink Away" folder in your cloud account. To take back the permission, remove Ink Away in your account's settings:
  - Google: [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
  - Dropbox: [dropbox.com/account/connected_apps](https://www.dropbox.com/account/connected_apps)

### Google user data

Ink Away's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Ink Away uses the data from your Google Drive only to sync your own Ink Away files between your own readers. It does not share it with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and no person reads it.

## Other network use

Checking for updates asks GitHub for the latest Ink Away release. Features that send something over the internet, such as converting handwriting to text with an online service you set up, say so where you turn them on and use only the service you choose.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date.
