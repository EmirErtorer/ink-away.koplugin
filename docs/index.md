# Ink Away

Ink Away is a free, open-source plugin for [KOReader](https://koreader.rocks) that lets you draw, write and annotate on your e-reader: notebooks, drawings, PDFs and the pages of your books.

- Source code and downloads: [github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin](https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin)
- [Privacy policy](privacy)
- Contact: emir1.ertorer@gmail.com

## Cloud sync

Ink Away can keep your notebooks the same on all your readers by copying them to your own Google Drive or Dropbox. You turn it on yourself, on the reader, by signing in on your phone with your usual account.

Ink Away asks Google only for the permission called `drive.file`. That permission lets Ink Away see, create, change and delete only the files that Ink Away itself made, in a folder called "Ink Away" in your My Drive. It cannot see any of your other files. Google words this permission as "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app", and that is all it covers.

Nothing you sync passes through any server of mine. Your reader talks directly to your own cloud account.
