Ink Away is a drawing, notes and book annotation app for KOReader, with **palm rejection** and **pen pressure** for stylus users.

* **Draw** with pens of every kind, watercolor and smudge, on up to five **layers**.
* **Take notes** in notebooks of lined, grid, dotted or planner pages, kept in folders and searchable, with handwriting turned into text.
* **Annotate any book** KOReader opens, from EPUB, MOBI and FB2 to PDF, DjVu and comics, with ink that follows its words when you change the font.

Export a transparent or white PNG, or a paged PDF, at your exact screen size.

## Features

### Toolbar

<img src="assets/readme-toolbar.svg" alt="The toolbar: Pen, Eraser, Shapes, Text, Image, Lasso, Undo, Redo, Settings, Library, File and Exit">

* File holds rename, duplicate, export, new drawing and new notebook. Zoom is the floating +/− control in the corner, with the Pan button just above it: tap it to pan, and again to go back to the tool you had.


### Write and draw

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pens** | Fineliner, Ballpoint, Fountain, Calligraphy with its own nib angle, Pencil, Highlighter, Marker, Watercolor and Smudge, each with size, opacity, greys everywhere and full colour on colour screens. The highlighter, marker and watercolor blend with the page, and text stays black under the highlighter. A brush maker makes your own. |
| **Pen case** | Up to 20 saved pens, each shown at its real size, with a floating strip of your first ones. |
| **Pen pressure** | On the Kindle Scribe and Kobo readers with a stylus the line follows how hard you press. |
| **Hold to straighten** | Hold the pen still at the end of a stroke and a rough line, box, ellipse or triangle snaps clean. Writing is left alone. |
| **Shapes** | Lines, curves, single and double arrows, rectangles, ellipses and triangles, filled or outlined, snapped to the grid or to 45°. A paint bucket fills an area in one tap. |
| **Eraser** | Clears ink back to transparent, or removes whole strokes. It leaves pictures alone unless you ask, and can spare text too. |
| **Text** | Text boxes in any installed font, with bold, italic, underline, highlight, lists and sizes, snapped to a notebook's lines. |
| **Pictures** | From a file or found online (Wikimedia or Openverse), then moved, resized, turned and cut out of their background. A picture can also sit behind a drawing to trace over. |
| **Symmetry** | Draw mirrored left to right, top to bottom, or four ways. |

### Layers

Sketch, ink and colour each on a layer of its own, as in a desktop or tablet drawing app.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Up to five** | File, Layers turns them on, and what you drew so far becomes the first layer. Drawings start without them, so nothing changes until you want them. |
| **The strip** | A small strip at the right, shown only while layers are on. Tap a layer to draw on it; tap it again to show or hide, rename, move, merge down or delete it. |
| **Colour under line art** | A stroke on a lower layer stays under the layers above it while you draw. The eraser, the lasso and the paint bucket only touch the layer you are on. |
| **Merge when done** | Merge a layer into the one under it, or turn layers off to merge them all into one drawing. Hidden layers stay out of exports. |
| **Light on your reader** | No layer is kept as a picture of its own, so drawing on layers is as fast as on a plain page and takes little memory. |

### Select and edit

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lasso** | Loop writing, shapes, pictures and text, then move, resize, turn, flip, recolour, change opacity or size, duplicate or delete them, or cut, copy and paste to any page of any notebook or drawing. With Pan, a touch picks a shape or picture. |
| **Convert to text** | Lassoed printed handwriting becomes a text box, read on the device (English letters and digits). One undo brings the writing back. |
| **Undo and redo** | From the toolbar, or with two fingers (see Gestures). |

### Notebooks and PDFs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Notebooks** | As many pages as you like, each with a title, a star and its own paper. Insert, duplicate and move pages, or save one as a template to start new pages from. |
| **Papers and planners** | Blank, lined, grid, dotted, isometric, margin ruled and Cornell, plus handwriting practice, checklist, two columns, storyboard, music, daily, weekly, week columns, monthly, meeting notes and a habit tracker. You set the line spacing and strength, and drawings can use any of them as a guide. |
| **Paper colours** | Twelve papers on colour screens, from cream and legal pad to kraft, blueprint and chalkboard, and white or black on grey ones. Black ink and text turn white on a dark paper, and exports come out on the same paper. |
| **Annotate PDFs** | Open any PDF as a notebook and write on its pages. |
| **Links and contents** | Make anything a link to a page of this or another notebook, with a Back button to return. A contents page lists the titled pages, each one a link. |

### Write on your books

| | |
|---|---|
| **Any book** | EPUB, MOBI, FB2, PDF, DjVu, CBZ and every other format KOReader opens. Pen, eraser, shapes, text and pictures work right on the page. |
| **Ink follows the text** | On a reflowing book each stroke is anchored to the words beside it, so it stays with them when you change the font size, margins or orientation. On PDFs and comics it keeps its place on the page. |
| **Smart highlighter** | A highlighter stroke over text becomes KOReader's own highlight in the pen's colour and shows in your highlights list. Strokes that wobble or run into the margin still snap; over empty space it stays ink. |
| **Book notes** | A notebook for each book with a page for each chapter, in a window over the page that expands to full screen. |
| **Toolbar** | On any side of the screen, and it folds away. |
| **Kept with the book** | Annotations live beside the book's own KOReader data and follow it when it is moved or renamed. Deleting them puts them in Ink Away's trash for 30 days. |

### Organize and find

|             |                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Library** | Every drawing and notebook is a file that saves itself. Folders, thumbnails, rename, move, duplicate and delete, all without a computer. |
| **Browse**  | A folder's notebooks as coloured tabs beside their pages, with a starred-only view. Move or copy pages to any notebook.                   |
| **Search**  | By name, or by the text inside pages, handwriting turned into text included.                                                             |
| **Trash**   | Deleted notebooks, drawings, folders and pages wait 30 days and go back exactly where they were if restored.                             |

### Export

| | |
|---|---|
| **PNG** | Transparent or white, at your screen size, of the whole page or an area you pick, with or without the grid. |
| **PDF** | A notebook, some of its pages, or a whole folder at once, with bookmarks for titled pages, working links and page numbers, on white or sandpaper paper. |
| **Bookshelf ornament** | With the Bookshelf plugin installed, save a drawing straight into its ornaments. |

### Pen, screen and colour

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dark mode** | Light, Dark or following KOReader's night mode, for Ink Away's toolbars, menus and library. Your page and colours are never changed. |
| **Palm rejection** | Rest your hand on the screen while the pen writes (on by default on Kindle Scribe and reMarkable). The pen can still tap the toolbar and menus. |
| **Stylus buttons** | The eraser end erases, and holding the side button makes the pen a highlighter, on pens that have them. You can set them to anything else. |
| **Colour screens** | A theme colour, Ink Away green by default, and colour ink that shows as you draw. |
| **E-ink** | Light refreshes while you draw, an optional clean-up refresh every few strokes, portrait or landscape, and bars that fold away for more room. |

### Help and updates

| | |
|---|---|
| **Guide** | Short cards on what a glance doesn't show, by topic, each with "Show me". |
| **Updates** | Settings, Updates shows the latest release and any newer prerelease apart from it, with their notes. It installs them after checking the download. Once a day, only on Wi-Fi that is already on, Ink Away looks by itself and puts a dot on the Settings button when there is something new. |
| **Test pen and touch** | Shows how your pen, its buttons and your hand arrive, handy for bug reports. |

### Gestures

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Two-finger tap | Undo |
| Two-finger double tap | Redo |
| Two-finger swipe left or right | Turn a notebook's page |
| Long two-finger swipe up | Open Browse in a notebook, or the Library from a drawing |
| Pinch | Zoom |
| Two-finger drag | Move around the page |
| Hold Prev or Next | Jump to the first or last page |
| Swipe down the right edge (in a book) | Annotate the book |
| Swipe up the right edge | The book's notes, or Ink Away outside a book |

Ink Away sets up the two book gestures for you where they are free, and tells you which you got; a reader that already uses the right edge gets diagonal swipes with two fingers instead. Every gesture and pen button can be changed in Settings, Gestures and pen buttons.

With palm rejection on, **Finger on the page** in the pen settings decides what a finger does while the pen writes:
* **Navigate** (scroll, turn pages, hold a picture or shape for its menu) or
* **Nothing**. A finger never draws then, so a resting hand can't either.


## Installation

<a href="https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin/releases/latest"><img src="assets/readme-download.svg" alt="Download the latest release"></a>

Unzip the download and copy the `ink-away.koplugin` folder inside it into KOReader's `plugins` directory:

- Kindle: `koreader/plugins/ink-away.koplugin/`
- Kobo: `.adds/koreader/plugins/ink-away.koplugin/`
- Android: `koreader/plugins/ink-away.koplugin/` in app storage

Then restart KOReader. Open from Top menu → **Tools** tab → "Ink Away (drawing canvas)" near the top. In a book the same tab has an Ink Away menu with Annotate the book, Book notes and Open Ink Away. You can also map them to gestures in KOReader's gesture manager.

From then on Ink Away can update itself: Settings, Updates.

What changed in each version is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).


## Notes

- Palm rejection needs KOReader 2026.07+.
- Drawings and notebooks are kept in an "ink away" folder and saved automatically; exported PNGs and PDFs go to "ink away/exports" unless you pick another folder. Work from older versions' "drawing projects" and "notebook projects" folders moves into the "ink away" folder on the first start.
- Settings → "When Ink Away opens" picks what you see first: the last document, the library, or Notebooks (the notebook browser on your last notebook, for note-taking).
- On e-ink, black ink refreshes faster than greys/white and a white pen is invisible on the white canvas, exports are unaffected.
